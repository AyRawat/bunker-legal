Privacy Policy

The Bunker is a study app that locks your phone while you study and records how long you studied. This policy explains what the app collects, why, where it goes, and how to delete it. We collect as little as the app needs to work. We show no ads, run no analytics or tracking, and never sell your data.

What we collect

  • Your sign-in account basics. When you sign in with Google or Apple we receive your name, email address, and an account identifier from that provider. This is only used to create your account and keep your records tied to you. If you use Apple's "Hide My Email", we only ever see the relay address.
  • Your study sessions. For each session: start and end time, planned length, whether it was completed, abandoned, or broken, and the label you chose (such as a subject, chapter, or task).
  • Which app interrupted a session. If you grant Usage Access and leave The Bunker during a session, the app records the name of the app you opened, so your session history can show why it broke. We do not collect your general app usage history.
  • A username, if you choose one. To add friends you pick a username. Friends must be added by exact username and accepted by both people; there is no search or suggestions. Once accepted, a friend can see your username, whether you are studying right now or when you last studied, and — only inside a challenge you both joined — your total minutes for that challenge. Nobody ever sees your session list, labels, or voice notes. You can leave the "studying now" status off in Friends.
  • A push notification token. If you allow notifications, the app stores a device token from Google's Firebase Cloud Messaging so we can tell you about friend requests and challenges. It identifies the app install, not you, and is removed when you sign out or delete your account.
  • Voice notes, only if you record one. After a session you can optionally record a short voice summary using the microphone. The microphone is never used otherwise. Voice notes are deleted automatically after 30 days.

What stays on your phone

Your tasks, schedule, reminders, goals, and settings are stored only on your device. The app's Do Not Disturb and exact-alarm permissions are used on your phone to silence it during a session and to remind you at the time you chose; nothing from them is sent to us.

Why we collect it

  • To sign you in and keep your study history when you reinstall or change phones.
  • To show your streaks, daily totals, and weekly and monthly reports.
  • To keep session records honest (a session you leave does not count).
  • To let friends you accepted see when you are studying and to score challenges.
  • To send notifications about friend requests and challenges, only when you have allowed them.

Where it is stored

Account and session data is stored with Supabase (our database and sign-in provider). Voice notes are stored with Cloudflare R2. Sign-in is handled by Google or Apple (your choice), and notifications are delivered by Google Firebase Cloud Messaging. These providers process data only on our behalf. Data is encrypted in transit, and database rules ensure each account can read only its own records. Some providers may store data on servers outside India.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with anyone for advertising or marketing. We disclose data only to the service providers above, or if required by law.

Students under 18

The Bunker is built for school students. If you are under 18, please use the app with the permission of a parent or guardian. A parent or guardian can contact us at any time to see or delete their child's data.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your account and session history while your account exists. Voice notes are deleted after 30 days. You can delete your account at any time:

  • In the app: open You, tap Delete account, then tap it again to confirm. Your account, synced study sessions, and voice notes are deleted immediately, and the app's data on your phone is cleared.
  • By email: if you no longer have the app, email us from the address you signed in with, with the subject "Delete my Bunker account". We will delete everything within 30 days and confirm by email.

Deleted data cannot be recovered.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct, or delete your personal data, or withdraw consent, by contacting us. Withdrawing consent means we delete your account, since the app cannot work without it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
